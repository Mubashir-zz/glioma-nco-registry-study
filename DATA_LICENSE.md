# Data licence and provenance

**Code** (`*.R`, `*.py`) is released under the MIT Licence — see `LICENSE`.

**Trial-level data** (`gbm_analytic_289.csv`, `GBM_RCT_GLOBAL_Matrix.xlsx`,
`revised_outputs/analysis_dataset.csv`, `revised_outputs/tables/*`) is released
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The underlying records are public registry entries from ClinicalTrials.gov,
ISRCTN, the EU Clinical Trials Register and WHO ICTRP national registries. The
registry text is public; the adjudication — inclusion decisions, outcome-domain
classification, sponsor class, and the objective-NCO determination for each
trial — is original work and is what the CC BY term covers. If you use it,
cite the study.

No patient-level data is included. Every record is a trial registration.
